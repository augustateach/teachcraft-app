Participle Clauses:
Three Structures to Master

Jak rozpoznać podmiot, stronę bierną i czynności towarzyszące.

01 · PASSIVE MEANING

Past participle clause

Podmiot jest odbiorcą czynności. W konstrukcji wprowadzającej i zdaniu głównym chodzi o ten sam podmiot.

The proposal, which was initially rejected, was eventually approved.
↓
Initially rejected, the proposal was eventually approved.
Full clauseParticiple clause
which was rejectedrejected
which was designeddesigned
which was severely damagedseverely damaged
which had been writtenwritten / having been written*

More examples

  • Originally designed as a hospital, the building now houses a museum.
  • Deeply disappointed by the decision, she handed in her resignation.
  • Widely criticised at first, the book eventually became a bestseller.
Ask yourself: Does the subject perform or receive the action?
The proposal receives the action → V3.
*Having been + V3 podkreśla wcześniejszą czynność bierną. Krótsze V3 wystarcza, gdy kolejność wynika z kontekstu.
02 · TWO DIFFERENT SUBJECTS

Absolute participle clause

Gdy obie części zdania mają różne podmioty, konstrukcja imiesłowowa może mieć własny podmiot.

Since all the arrangements had been made, the conference could begin.
↓
With all the arrangements having been made, the conference could begin.
SUBJECT 1the arrangements
had been made
SUBJECT 2the conference
could begin

Three correct versions

With all the arrangements having been made, the conference could begin.

All the arrangements having been made, the conference could begin.

With all the arrangements made, the conference could begin.

Ostatnia wersja jest najbardziej zwięzła. Pierwsze dwie wyraźniej zaznaczają wcześniejszą czynność.

More examples

  • With all the documents signed, the agreement could come into effect.
  • With the technical problems resolved, the presentation could begin.
  • With all the evidence having been collected, the investigation entered its final stage.
Common mistake: dangling participle
Having made all the arrangements, the conference could begin.
Kto poczynił przygotowania? Zdanie sugeruje, że konferencja. To błąd logiczny.
03 · ACCOMPANYING CIRCUMSTANCES

With + noun + -ing

Konstrukcja opisuje czynność towarzyszącą głównemu wydarzeniu, często z własnym podmiotem.

Two officers accompanied the suspect as he entered the courtroom.
↓
The suspect entered the courtroom with two officers accompanying him.
MAIN ACTIONThe suspect entered the courtroom.
ACCOMPANYING ACTIONTwo officers were accompanying him.
WITH + NOUN / PRONOUN + V-ing

More examples

  • The politician left the building with several journalists following her.
  • She delivered her speech with everyone watching her closely.
  • The winner walked onto the stage with the audience applauding.
  • He tried to concentrate with his neighbours arguing next door.
Who performs the additional action?
The officers accompany. The journalists follow. The audience applauds. Podmiot po with wykonuje czynność → V-ing.

Grammar at a glance

StructureFunction / example
V3Passive, shared subject: Initially rejected, the proposal...
With + noun + V3Resulting state: With the work completed...
With + noun + having been + V3Earlier passive action: With the work having been completed...
With + noun + V-ingAccompanying active action: With everyone watching...

How to choose

SubjectsActivePassive
Same subjectV-ing / having + V3V3 / having been + V3
Different subjectsWith + noun + V-ing (simultaneous)With + noun + V3 / having been + V3

Remember: Identify the subject, decide whether the meaning is active or passive, and establish the relationship between the actions. The table focuses on the constructions practised here.

Precision first

This review checks whether you can choose vocabulary that is not merely grammatical, but exact and natural in a scientific context.

Teacher notes / model points

Sterilise means removing or destroying microorganisms, not merely making something clean.

Corrode names gradual chemical damage, especially to metal.

Freeze names the change of state from liquid to solid due to low temperature.

Displace means forcing a substance, such as water, out of the space it occupied.

Relative Clauses: Who, Which, Whose, Where, When

Play the warm-up game first. Then do Tasks 2–6 and press Check answers. You can correct your mistakes and check again.

TASK 1

Who am I? – Pronoun Ladder. Guess the job. Each clue uses a different relative pronoun. Fewer clues = more points.

Points: guess after clue 1 = 5 points, after clue 2 = 4 … after clue 5 = 1.
Twist – Grammar Steal: in every ladder, one clue has a grammar mistake. Click it and press Steal for +1 point.

            Tip: in Tasks 5–6 write the full sentence. Punctuation matters only for commas.

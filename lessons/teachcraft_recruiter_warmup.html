ROUND 1 · DISCUSS IN PAIRS · 2 MIN

Meet the candidates

Who would YOU hire? Give two reasons. Be ready to defend your decision.
ROUND 2 · CLASS VOTE

Cast your vote

One vote per student. Tap +1 VOTE for the candidate your classmate chooses.

Ask: Which weakness are you willing to accept — and why?
ROUND 3 · NEW INFORMATION

Plot twist!

URGENT UPDATE FROM HR

THE JOB HAS CHANGED.

The new employee must work with international clients, learn unfamiliar software quickly and solve unexpected problems under pressure.

Would you still hire the same person?

Convince someone who voted differently to change their mind.
ROUND 4 · VOTE AGAIN

Have you changed your mind?

Vote again using the new job requirements.

Use the same number of voters in both rounds. Then compare the results.
ROUND 5 · THE RESULTS

Before vs after

Which candidate gained or lost support? What changed your minds?
ROUND 6 · GUESS THE DATA

What do employers value?

Guess the percentages. Tap a question mark to reveal the answer.

Source: figures printed in the Work and Learn textbook extract (historical survey figures, not current labour-market statistics).
